**Bug found on the first live run, fixed, recorded in `ai/memory.md`:** the tier-expansion job rescheduled its own successor from inside its own BullMQ processor, reusing the same job id. Tier 1 fired correctly and tier 2 silently vanished, with no error anywhere. ## Not engineering, and easy to underestimate

These block launch as firmly as any code.

- [ ] Register the business entity
- [ ] Razorpay account, KYC, Route activation
- [ ] Nodal or escrow arrangement (**legally required to pool user funds**)
- [ ] Chartered accountant review of GST, TCS and TDS handling
- [ ] Google Maps Platform key
- [ ] SMS provider with DLT registration
- [ ] Domain, DNS, email sender identity
- [ ] Terms of service, privacy policy, worker agreement
- [ ] **Recruit and verify 20 workers in Kolkata.** The marketplace does not work without supply, and no code substitutes for this
- [ ] Decide the cancellation compensation rate
- [ ] Consider the concierge pilot: broker tasks manually over WhatsApp first, to test willingness to pay before depending on the platform
